The '''2025–26 UEFA Conference League league phase''' began on 2 October and ended on 18 December 2025. A total of 36 teams competed in the league phase to decide the 24 places in the [[2025–26 UEFA Conference League knockout phase|knockout phase]] of the [[2025–26 UEFA Conference League]].

[[Crystal Palace F.C.|Crystal Palace]], [[FC Drita|Drita]], [[Hamrun Spartans F.C.|Hamrun Spartans]], [[Kuopion Palloseura|KuPS]], [[Rayo Vallecano]], [[Samsunspor]], [[Shelbourne F.C.|Shelbourne]], [[KF Shkëndija|Shkëndija]], [[SK Sigma Olomouc|Sigma Olomouc]] and [[CS Universitatea Craiova|Universitatea Craiova]] made their debut appearances in a major UEFA competition group or league phase. Hamrun Spartans was the first team from Malta to play in a major UEFA competition group stage or league phase.<ref>{{cite web |date=28 August 2025 |title=Ħamrun Spartans make history as first Maltese team to reach UEFA league phase |url=https://timesofmalta.com/article/hamrun-spartans-make-history-first-maltese-team-uefa-league-phase.1115363 |access-date=28 August 2025 |website=Times of Malta}}</ref>

A total of 29 national associations were represented in the league phase. Poland was the first country to have four clubs play in the Conference League group stage/league phase, and it was also the first time in history that four Polish clubs competed in any edition of a UEFA competition group stage or league phase.

==Format==
Each team played six matches, three at home and three away, against six different opponents, with all 36 teams ranked in a single league table. Teams were separated into six pots based on their [[UEFA coefficient#Men's club coefficient|2025 UEFA club coefficients]], and each team played one team from each of the six pots. The top eight teams in the standings received a [[Bye (sports)|bye]] to the round of 16. The teams ranked from 9th to 24th contested the knockout phase play-offs, with the teams ranked from 9th to 16th seeded for the draw. Teams ranked from 25th to 36th were eliminated from European competition.

===Tiebreakers===
Teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, 0 points for a loss). If two or more teams were equal on points upon completion of the league phase, the following tiebreaking criteria were applied, in the order given, to determine their rankings:<ref name="regulations">{{cite web |url=https://documents.uefa.com/r/Regulations-of-the-UEFA-Conference-League-2025/26-Online |title=Regulations of the UEFA Conference League – 2025/26 Season |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=27 June 2025 |access-date=27 August 2025 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250911174042/https://documents.uefa.com/api/khub/maps/lF5ByLxUQ80KExi2jpZBUw/attachments/hNUmn2jBcBnQU7XzDPWCOw-lF5ByLxUQ80KExi2jpZBUw/content |archive-date=11 September 2025}}</ref>

While the league phase was in progress, teams were ranked according to criteria 1–5 only, and if still tied, were given equal ranking and sorted alphabetically. Criteria 6–10 were only used after the final matches took place.

==Teams and seeding==
The 36 teams were divided into six pots of six teams each, with teams allocated to pots based on their 2025 [[UEFA coefficient#Men's club coefficient|UEFA club coefficients]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.uefa.com/uefaconferenceleague/news/029c-1e974a3bed51-1f9607a14f3c-1000/ |title=League phase draw pots confirmed for the 2025/26 Conference League |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=28 August 2025 |access-date=29 August 2025}}</ref><ref name="club coefficients">{{cite web |url=https://www.uefa.com/nationalassociations/uefarankings/club/?year=2025 |title=Club coefficients |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=27 August 2025 |access-date=27 August 2025}}</ref> The participants included:
*24 winners of the [[2025–26 UEFA Conference League#Play-off round|play-off round]] (5 from the [[2025–26 UEFA Conference League qualifying (third and play-off round matches)#Champions Path matches 2|Champions Path]], 19 from the [[2025–26 UEFA Conference League qualifying (third and play-off round matches)#Main Path matches 2|Main Path]])
*12 losers of the [[2025–26 UEFA Europa League#Play-off round|Europa League play-off round]]

==Draw==
The draw for the league phase pairings was held at the [[Grimaldi Forum]] in [[Monaco]] on 29 August 2025, 13:00 [[Central European Summer Time|CEST]], along with the draw for the [[2025–26 UEFA Europa League league phase|Europa League league phase]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.uefa.com/uefaconferenceleague/draws/2026/2002120/ |title=UEFA Conference League: League phase draw |publisher=[[UEFA]] |access-date=28 August 2025}}</ref> The automated draw software instantly conducted the full digital draw at random, determining the home and away matches for all clubs. Each team faced one opponent from each of the six pots, three of which they faced at home and three away. For the purposes of determining match venues, adjacent pots were paired (Pot 1 with 2, 3 with 4, and 5 with 6) so that, for each pot pairing, teams would face one opponent at home and one away. Teams could not face opponents from their own association, and could only be drawn against a maximum of two sides from the same association. For political reasons, clubs from [[Status of Gibraltar|Gibraltar–Spain]] and [[Bosnia and Herzegovina–Kosovo relations|Kosovo–Bosnia and Herzegovina]] could not be drawn against each other. The draw results were then revealed pot-by-pot, starting with Pot 1, with the intention of "maintaining suspense and clarity for viewers". This was a change of format from the previous season, where teams were individually drawn before their opponents were digitally determined.<ref name="procedure">{{cite web |url=https://editorial.uefa.com/resources/029c-1e97e465f313-a2b58db2ada2-1000/uecl_league_phase_draw_procedure.pdf |title=UEFA Conference League draw procedure: League phase |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=29 August 2025 |access-date=29 August 2025}}</ref><ref name="league phase draws">{{cite news |url=https://www.uefa.com/news-media/news/029c-1e83efbf2042-ce3c225dc1bd-1000/ |title=UEFA club competition league phase draws to take place in Monaco on 28 and 29 August |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=19 August 2025 |access-date=27 August 2025}}</ref>

==Matches==
The fixture list was announced on 30 August 2025, the day after the draw.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.uefa.com/uefaconferenceleague/news/029c-1e9ad66c8169-aaecb38941a7-1000/ |title=2025/26 Conference League: All the league phase fixtures |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=30 August 2025 |access-date=30 August 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://editorial.uefa.com/resources/029c-1e9ae5ea49ac-7cae1ab0c8c4-1000/uefa_conference_league_-_2025-26_-_league_phase_fixtures_by_matchday.pdf |title=2025/26 UEFA Conference League: League phase match calendar (by matchday) |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=30 August 2025 |access-date=30 August 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://editorial.uefa.com/resources/029c-1e9ae68b580f-5d893f7675af-1000/uefa_conference_league_-_2025-26_-_league_phase_fixtures_by_pot.pdf |title=2025/26 UEFA Conference League: League phase match calendar (by pot) |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=30 August 2025 |access-date=30 August 2025}}</ref> This was to ensure no calendar clashes with teams in the [[2025–26 UEFA Champions League league phase|Champions League]] and [[2025–26 UEFA Europa League league phase|Europa League]] playing in the same cities.

In principle, each team did not play more than two home matches or two away matches in a row, and played one home match and one away match across both the first and last two matchdays. The matches were played on 2 October, 23 October, 6 November, 27 November, 11 December and 18 December 2025 (exclusive week).{{refn|group=note|As part of the scheduling for the 2025–26 UEFA men's club season, each competition had an "exclusive week" in the calendar, with no other competitions scheduled during this week. For the Conference League, this took place on matchday 6 (18 December 2025).}} All matches were generally played on Thursdays. In principle, the scheduled kick-off times were 16:30, 18:45 and 21:00 CET/CEST. All fixtures on the final matchday were played simultaneously at 21:00.<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/0268-12157d69ce2d-9f011c70f6fa-1000/ |title=New format for Champions League post-2025: Everything you need to know |website=[[UEFA]] |publisher=Union of European Football Associations |date=12 June 2025 |access-date=27 August 2025}}</ref><ref name="regulations"/>

Times are [[Central European Time|CET]] or [[Central European Summer Time|CEST]],{{refn|group=note|[[Central European Summer Time|CEST]] ([[UTC+02:00|UTC+2]]) for dates up to 25 October 2025 (matchdays 1–2), and [[Central European Time|CET]] ([[UTC+01:00|UTC+1]]) for dates thereafter (matchdays 3–6).}} as listed by UEFA (local times, if different, are in parentheses).
