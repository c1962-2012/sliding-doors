The '''2026–27 UEFA Conference League league phase''' began on 15 October 2026 and will end on 17 December 2026. A total of 36 teams are competing in the league phase to decide the 24 places in the [[2026–27 UEFA Conference League knockout phase|knockout phase]] of the [[2026–27 UEFA Conference League]].

[[Aarhus Gymnastikforening|AGF]], [[KF Egnatia|Egnatia]], [[FC Iberia 1999|Iberia 1999]], [[Inter Club d'Escaldes]], [[FK Kauno Žalgiris|Kauno Žalgiris]], [[Mjällby AIF]], [[Riga FC|Riga]] and [[Sint-Truidense VV|Sint-Truiden]] are making their debut appearances in a major UEFA competition group stage or league phase. Inter Club d'Escaldes became the first team from Andorra to play in a major UEFA competition group or league phase.

A total of 30 national associations are being represented in the league phase.

==Format==
===Tiebreakers===
Teams are ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, 0 points for a loss). If two or more teams are equal on points upon completion of the league phase, the following tiebreaking criteria will be applied, in the order given, to determine their rankings:<ref name="regulations">{{cite web |url=https://documents.uefa.com/r/Regulations-of-the-UEFA-Conference-League-2026/27-Online |title=Regulations of the UEFA Conference League – 2026/27 Season |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=29 July 2026 |access-date=28 August 2026 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260828164820/https://documents.uefa.com/r/Regulations-of-the-UEFA-Conference-League-2026/27-Online|archive-date=28 August 2026}}</ref>

While the league phase is in progress, teams are ranked according to criteria 1–5 only, and if still tied, will be given equal ranking and sorted alphabetically. Criteria 6–10 will only be used after the final matches take place.

==Teams and seeding==
The 36 teams were divided into six pots of six teams each, with teams allocated to pots based on their 2026 [[UEFA coefficient#Men's club coefficient|UEFA club coefficients]]. The participants include:
*24 winners of the [[2026–27 UEFA Conference League#Play-off round|play-off round]] (5 from the [[2026–27 UEFA Conference League qualifying (third and play-off round matches)#Champions Path matches 2|Champions Path]], 19 from the [[2026–27 UEFA Conference League qualifying (third and play-off round matches)#Main Path matches 2|Main Path]])
*12 losers of the [[2026–27 UEFA Europa League#Play-off round|Europa League play-off round]]
