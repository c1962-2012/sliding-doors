The '''2026–27 UEFA Europa League league phase''' began on 16 September 2026 and will end on 28 January 2027. A total of 36 teams are competing in the league phase to decide the 24 places in the [[2026–27 UEFA Europa League knockout phase|knockout phase]] of the [[2026–27 UEFA Europa League]].

[[FC Ararat-Armenia|Ararat-Armenia]], [[AFC Bournemouth|Bournemouth]], [[NK Celje|Celje]], [[Crystal Palace F.C.|Crystal Palace]], [[Jagiellonia Białystok]], [[Lillestrøm SK|Lillestrøm]], [[NEC Nijmegen|NEC]], [[OFI Crete F.C.|OFI]], [[Sunderland A.F.C.|Sunderland]] and [[S.C.U. Torreense|Torreense]] are making their debut appearances since the introduction of the group stage (although NEC had previously appeared in the UEFA Cup group stage). Ararat-Armenia, Bournemouth, Lillestrøm, OFI, Sunderland and Torreense are making their debut appearances in a major UEFA competition group or league phase, with Bournemouth and Torreense qualifying for European football for the first time in their history. Ararat-Armenia became the first team from Armenia to play in the Europa League group stage or league phase. 

A total of 22 national associations are being represented in the league phase.

==Format==
Each team will play eight matches, four at home and four away, against eight different opponents, with all 36 teams ranked in a single league table (known as the [[Swiss-system tournament|Swiss system]]). Teams were separated into four pots based on their [[UEFA coefficient#Men's club coefficient|2026 UEFA club coefficients]], and each team will play two teams from each of the four pots – one at home and one away. The top eight teams in the standings will receive a [[Bye (sports)|bye]] to the round of 16. The teams ranked from 9th to 24th will contest the knockout phase play-offs, with the teams ranked from 9th to 16th seeded for the draw. Teams ranked from 25th to 36th will be eliminated from European competition.

===Tiebreakers===
Teams are ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, 0 points for a loss). If two or more teams are equal on points upon completion of the league phase, the following tiebreaking criteria will be applied, in the order given, to determine their rankings:<ref name="regulations">{{cite web |url=https://documents.uefa.com/r/Regulations-of-the-UEFA-Europa-League-2026/27-Online |title=Regulations of the UEFA Europa League – 2026/27 Season |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=29 July 2026 |access-date=28 August 2026 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260828164820/https://documents.uefa.com/r/Regulations-of-the-UEFA-Europa-League-2026/27-Online|archive-date=28 August 2026}}</ref>

While the league phase is in progress, teams are ranked according to criteria 1–5 only, and if still tied, will be given equal ranking and sorted alphabetically. Criteria 6–10 will only be used after the final matches take place.

==Teams and seeding==
The 36 teams were divided into four pots of nine teams each, with teams allocated to pots based on their 2026 [[UEFA coefficient#Men's club coefficient|UEFA club coefficients]]. The participants include:
*13 teams which entered at this stage
*12 winners of the [[2026–27 UEFA Europa League qualifying#Play-off round|play-off round]]
*7 losers of the [[2026–27 UEFA Champions League qualifying#Play-off round|Champions League play-off round]] (5 from the [[2026–27 UEFA Champions League qualifying#Champions Path matches 3|Champions Path]], 2 from the [[2026–27 UEFA Champions League qualifying#League Path matches 3|League Path]])
*4 losers of the [[2026–27 UEFA Champions League qualifying#League Path matches 2|Champions League third qualifying round (League Path)]]

==Draw==
The draw for the league phase pairings was held at the [[Grimaldi Forum]] in [[Monaco]] on 28 August 2026, 13:00 [[Central European Summer Time|CEST]], along with the draw for the [[2026–27 UEFA Conference League league phase|Conference League league phase]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.uefa.com/uefaeuropaleague/draws/2027/2002229/ |title=League phase draw |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=28 August 2026 |access-date=28 August 2026}}</ref> The automated draw software instantly conducted the full digital draw at random, determining the home and away matches for all clubs. Each team faced two opponents from each of the four pots, one of which they faced at home and one away. Teams could not face opponents from their own association, and could only be drawn against a maximum of two sides from the same association. In addition, any individual fixture between two teams that had already been drawn in the last two seasons, could not be repeated for the third time with the same home team. The draw results were then be revealed pot-by-pot, starting with Pot 1, with the intention of "maintaining suspense and clarity for viewers".

==League phase table==
The top eight ranked teams receive a bye directly to the round of 16. Teams ranked from 9th to 24th contest the knockout phase play-offs, with teams ranked from 9th to 16th seeded for the draw, while those ranked from 17th to 24th are not. Teams ranked from 25th to 36th are eliminated from all competitions.
