The '''2025–26 UEFA Conference League knockout phase''' began on 19 February with the knockout phase play-offs and ended on 27 May 2026 with the [[2026 UEFA Conference League final|final]] at the [[Red Bull Arena (Leipzig)|Red Bull Arena]] in [[Leipzig]], Germany, to decide the champions of the [[2025–26 UEFA Conference League]].<ref name="schedule">{{cite news |url=https://www.uefa.com/uefaconferenceleague/news/0296-1d21e9c765e0-02d32ee0d103-1000--2025-26-conference-league-format-dates-draws-final/|title=2025/26 UEFA Conference League: Matches, final, key dates |publisher=UEFA |date=26 May 2025 |access-date=13 June 2025}}</ref> A total of 24 teams competed in the knockout phase, with 16 entering in the play-offs and 8 receiving a [[Bye (sports)|bye]] to the round of 16.<ref name="regulations">{{cite web |url=https://documents.uefa.com/r/Regulations-of-the-UEFA-Conference-League-2025/26-Online |title=Regulations of the UEFA Conference League – 2025/26 Season |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=27 June 2025 |access-date=27 August 2025 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250911174042/https://documents.uefa.com/api/khub/maps/lF5ByLxUQ80KExi2jpZBUw/attachments/hNUmn2jBcBnQU7XzDPWCOw-lF5ByLxUQ80KExi2jpZBUw/content |archive-date=11 September 2025}}</ref>

==Qualified teams==
The knockout phase involved the top 24 teams that qualified from the [[2025–26 UEFA Conference League league phase|league phase]]. The top 8 teams received a [[Bye (sports)|bye]] to the round of 16, while teams finishing in positions 9 to 24 entered the knockout phase play-offs.

==Format==
Each tie in the [[Single-elimination tournament|knockout phase]], apart from the final, was played over [[Two-legged tie|two legs]], with each team playing one leg at home. The team that scored more goals on aggregate over the two legs advanced to the next round. If the aggregate score was level, then 30 minutes of [[Overtime (sports)#Association football|extra time]] were played (the [[away goals rule]] was not applied). If the score was still level at the end of extra time, the winners were decided by a [[Penalty shoot-out (association football)|penalty shoot-out]]. In the final, which was played as a single match, if the score was level at the end of normal time, extra time was played, followed by a penalty shoot-out if the score was still level.<ref name="regulations"/>

===Draw procedure===
<section begin=Format />
In the knockout phase, there was no country protection, with teams from the same association able to face each other in any round. Teams could also face opponents they played during the league phase.

The mechanism of the draws for each round was as follows:<ref name="regulations"/>
*In the draw for the knockout phase play-offs, the eight teams finishing the league phase in positions 9–16 were seeded, and the eight teams finishing the league phase in positions 17–24 were unseeded. The draw was split into four sections based on the predetermined bracket, with the seeded teams in each section drawn against one of their two possible unseeded opponents. The seeded teams hosted the second leg.
*In the draw for the round of 16, the eight teams finishing the league phase in positions 1–8 were seeded, and the eight winners of the knockout phase play-offs were unseeded. Again, the draw was split into four sections based on the predetermined bracket, with the seeded teams in each section drawn against one of their two possible unseeded opponents. The seeded teams hosted the second leg.

In the quarter-finals and semi-finals, both the exact match pairings and order of legs were predetermined based on the tournament bracket. The teams with the better league phase ranking played the second leg of each round at home if they continued advancing. Should a seeded team have been beaten, the team that eliminated them would take over their seeding position. The winner of semi-final 1 was designated as the "home" team for the final (for administrative purposes, as it was played at a neutral venue).<section end=Format />

In the knockout phase, teams from the same or nearby cities were not scheduled to play at home on the same day or on consecutive days, due to logistics and crowd control.<ref name="regulations"/>{{rp|at=Art. 24.02}} To avoid such scheduling conflict, if the two teams were drawn to play at home for the same leg, the home match of the team that had the lower league phase ranking (if in the same competition) or the team playing in lower tier competition (Conference League) was moved from Thursday from a regularly scheduled time to an earlier time slot, to a different day, and/or at an alternative venue without clashing any other competition. However, a fixture reversal only applied if a team qualified to play the second leg at home decided to inform the UEFA administration before the draw to reverse the tie and play the first leg at home and not a second leg at an alternative venue.<ref>{{cite web |title=Principles set by the UEFA Club Competitions Committee for the 2025/26 season – Stadium and City Clashes |url=https://editorial.uefa.com/resources/02a0-1f66531ca666-12390a1116bd-1000/club_competitions_committee_-_principles_for_stadium_city_clashes_2025-26.pdf}}</ref>

===Predetermined pairings===
The bracket structure for the knockout phase was partially fixed in advance using [[Seeding (sports)|seeding]], with a symmetrical pattern on both sides.<ref name="regulations"/> Teams' positions in the bracket were determined by their final standings in the league phase, ensuring that higher-ranked teams faced lower-ranked opponents in earlier rounds. As a result, certain sets of teams, such as the top two from the league phase, could not meet until the final.

The structure of each side of the bracket can be summarised as follows, with the exact pairings of the play-offs and round of 16 determined by a draw:{{refn|group=note|The draws determined the exact play-off and round of 16 pairings for each side of the bracket, which mirrored each other. For example, if the team in 9th was drawn against 23rd in the play-offs, the team in 10th would be drawn against 24th on the other side of the bracket.}}

**Pairing III: 13/14 vs 19/20

**Pairing D: 7/8 vs Winner I
*Quarter-finals

==Knockout phase play-offs==
<section begin=KPP />
The draw for the knockout phase play-offs was held on 16 January 2026, 13:00 [[Central European Time|CET]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.uefa.com/uefaconferenceleague/draws/2026/2002121/ |title=Knockout round play-off draw |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=16 January 2026 |access-date=30 January 2026}}</ref><section end=KPP />

===Seeding===
The draw was split into four seeded and four unseeded pots, based on the predetermined pairings for the knockout phase. Teams were allocated based on their final position in the [[2025–26 UEFA Conference League league phase|league phase]]. Teams in positions 9 to 16 were seeded (playing the second legs at home), while teams in positions 17 to 24 were unseeded. The draw began with the unseeded teams, allocating them all to a tie. Once completed, all the seeded teams were drawn into a tie as their opponents. For political reasons, clubs from [[Status of Gibraltar|Gibraltar–Spain]] and [[Bosnia and Herzegovina–Kosovo relations|Kosovo–Bosnia and Herzegovina]] could not be drawn against each other. Consequently, [[HŠK Zrinjski Mostar|Zrinjski Mostar]] and [[FC Drita|Drita]] had to be drawn into opposite sides of the bracket.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://editorial.uefa.com/resources/02a1-1fb1a1dbc6c1-86d8f408fa20-1000/uecl_ko_po_draw_procedure_-_final.pdf |title=UEFA Conference League – Draw procedure, Knockout phase play-offs |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=16 January 2026 |access-date=30 January 2026}}</ref>

==Round of 16==
<section begin=R16 />
The draw for the round of 16 was held on 27 February 2026, 14:00 [[Central European Time|CET]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.uefa.com/uefaconferenceleague/draws/2026/2002122/ |title=Round of 16 draw |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=27 February 2026 |access-date=27 February 2026}}</ref><section end=R16 />

===Seeding===
As the bracket was fixed, the draw contained only four seeded pots, based on the predetermined pairings for the knockout phase, with the top eight teams allocated based on their final position in the [[2025–26 UEFA Conference League league phase|league phase]]. Teams in positions 1 to 8 were seeded (playing the second legs at home), while the bracket positions of the winners of the knockout phase play-offs (unseeded) were predetermined. The top eight teams were drawn into the bracket against one of their two possible opponents.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://editorial.uefa.com/resources/02a2-20061ba8179b-ff3dc2d22298-1000/uecl_round_of_16_draw_procedure_-_final.pdf |title=UEFA Conference League – Draw procedure, Round of 16 / Quarter-finals / Semi-finals / Final |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=27 February 2026 |access-date=27 February 2026}}</ref>
