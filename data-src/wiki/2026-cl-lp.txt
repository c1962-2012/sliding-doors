The '''2025–26 UEFA Champions League league phase''' began on 16 September 2025 and ended on 28 January 2026. A total of 36 teams competed in the league phase to decide the 24 places in the [[2025–26 UEFA Champions League knockout phase|knockout phase]] of the [[2025–26 UEFA Champions League]].

[[FK Bodø/Glimt|Bodø/Glimt]], [[FC Kairat|Kairat]], [[Pafos FC|Pafos]] and [[Royale Union Saint-Gilloise|Union Saint-Gilloise]] made their debut appearances in the league phase/group stage, with Kairat, based in [[Almaty]], becoming the easternmost team to ever qualify for the Champions League since the introduction of the group stage.<ref>{{cite news |last=Kenmare |first=Jack |url=https://www.sportbible.com/football/football-news/champions-league/champions-league-kairat-almaty-celtic-scottish-premiership-kazakhstan-855308-20250826 |title=Kairat Almaty create piece of Champions League history after win over Celtic and it may never be topped |publisher=SPORTbible |date=26 August 2025}}</ref> In addition, Bodø/Glimt, based in [[Bodø (town)|Bodø]] within the [[Arctic Circle]], became the northernmost European team to ever compete in the Champions League.<ref>{{cite web |last=Rajan |first=Adwaidh |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/football/articles/c80dnj4nxkzo |title=Pafos, Bodo/Glimt join Kairat among CL debutants |publisher=[[BBC Sport]] |date=26 August 2025}}</ref> This marked the first time a Norwegian team participated in the competition proper since the [[2007–08 UEFA Champions League|2007–08 season]].<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/029c-1e931fa13243-5c80263021e5-1000/ |title=Champions League play-off second legs round-up: Bodø/Glimt, Kairat Almaty, Pafos through to league phase |publisher=UEFA |date=26 August 2025 |access-date=27 August 2025}}</ref> This was also the first season since [[2005–06 UEFA Champions League|2005–06]] in which no Ukrainian sides qualified for the group stage/league phase.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://112.ua/en/ukraina-vperse-za-20-rokiv-ne-bude-predstavlena-v-lizi-cempioniv-77402 |title=Dynamo eliminated from the Champions League: Ukraine without a group stage for the first time in 20 years. |publisher=112 Ukraine |date=12 August 2025}}</ref> After being drawn to play away against [[Sporting CP]], Kairat broke the record for the longest trip in UEFA competitions, travelling {{convert|6,911|km}} from Almaty to [[Lisbon]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/411285-farthest-distance-travelled-between-competitors-in-the-football-soccer-uefa-cha |title=Farthest distance travelled between competitors in the football (soccer) UEFA Champions League |publisher=Guinness World Records |date=18 September 2025 }}</ref>

A total of 16 national associations were represented in the league phase.

==Format==
Each team played eight matches, four at home and four away, against eight different opponents, with all 36 teams ranked in a single league table. Teams were separated into four pots based on their [[UEFA coefficient#Men's club coefficient|2025 UEFA club coefficients]], and each team played two teams from each of the four pots – one at home and one away. The top eight teams in the standings received a [[Bye (sports)|bye]] to the round of 16. The teams ranked from 9th to 24th contested the knockout phase play-offs, with the teams ranked from 9th to 16th seeded for the draw. Teams ranked from 25th to 36th were eliminated from European competition.

===Tiebreakers===
Teams were ranked according to [[Points (association football)|points]] (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, 0 points for a loss). If two or more teams were equal on points upon completion of the league phase, the following tiebreaking criteria were applied, in the order given, to determine their rankings:<ref name="regulations">{{cite web |url=https://documents.uefa.com/r/Regulations-of-the-UEFA-Champions-League-2025/26-Online |title=Regulations of the UEFA Champions League – 2025/26 Season |publisher=UEFA |date=27 June 2025 |access-date=27 August 2025 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250911174633/https://documents.uefa.com/api/khub/maps/1dewT2NITNj_nBcX3kbINQ/attachments/ZNwD1mfsugz1wXhvTSEDhA-1dewT2NITNj_nBcX3kbINQ/content |archive-date=11 September 2025}}</ref>

While the league phase was in progress, teams were ranked according to criteria 1–5 only, and if still tied, were given equal ranking and sorted alphabetically. Criteria 6–10 were only used after the final matches took place.<ref name="UCL table"/>

==Teams and seeding==
The 36 teams were divided into four pots of nine teams each, with the Champions League title holders automatically placed as the top seed of pot 1. All remaining teams were allocated to pots based on their 2025 [[UEFA coefficient#Men's club coefficient|UEFA club coefficients]].<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/029c-1e954c3512ef-8b9c6fa0f83b-1000/ |title=Champions League: League phase draw pots confirmed |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=27 August 2025 |access-date=28 August 2025}}</ref><ref name="club coefficients">{{cite web |url=https://www.uefa.com/nationalassociations/uefarankings/club/?year=2025 |title=Club coefficients |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=27 August 2025 |access-date=27 August 2025}}</ref> The participants included:
*29 teams which entered at this stage
*7 winners of the [[2025–26 UEFA Champions League qualifying#Play-off round|play-off round]] (5 from the [[2025–26 UEFA Champions League qualifying#Champions Path matches 3|Champions Path]], 2 from the [[2025–26 UEFA Champions League qualifying#League Path matches 3|League Path]])

==Draw==
The draw for the league phase pairings was held at the [[Grimaldi Forum]] in [[Monaco]] on 28 August 2025, 18:00 [[Central European Summer Time|CEST]], with [[Zlatan Ibrahimović]], the [[UEFA President's Award|UEFA President's Awardee]], and [[Kaká]] as drawing guests.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/draws/2026/2002110/ |title=UEFA Champions League: League phase draw |publisher=[[UEFA]] |access-date=27 August 2025}}</ref> All 36 teams were manually drawn using physical balls. For every team manually drawn, a guest on stage pressed a button for the automated software to digitally draw their opponents at random, determining which of their matches would be at home and which ones away. Each team faced two opponents from each of the four pots, one of which they faced at home and one away. Teams could not face opponents from their own association, and could only be drawn against a maximum of two sides from the same association. The draw started with Pot 1, assigning opponents to all teams, one after the other, and continued with the other pots in descending order until all teams were assigned their opponents.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://editorial.uefa.com/resources/029c-1e95e1cbbcd9-9ddb0c4f9a94-1000/ucl_league_phase_draw_procedure.pdf |title=UEFA Champions League draw procedure: League phase |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=28 August 2025 |access-date=28 August 2025}}</ref><ref name="league phase draws">{{cite news |url=https://www.uefa.com/news-media/news/029c-1e83efbf2042-ce3c225dc1bd-1000/ |title=UEFA club competition league phase draws to take place in Monaco on 28 and 29 August |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=19 August 2025 |access-date=27 August 2025}}</ref> Due to the number of English clubs competing in the League Phase, all non-English clubs in Pot 1 were required to draw two English fixtures to avoid deadlocking the draw.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/45356204/champions-league-draw-pot-1-teams-play-two-english-clubs|title=Champions League: Pot 1 teams must play two English clubs|publisher=ESPN|date=28 May 2025|access-date=28 May 2025}}</ref>

==Matches==
The fixture list was announced on 30 August 2025, two days after the draw.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/029c-1e9a2f63fe2d-ebf9ad643892-1000/ |title=2025/26 Champions League: All the league phase fixtures |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=30 August 2025 |access-date=30 August 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://editorial.uefa.com/resources/029c-1e9a3bf32351-6af9bd65aa7c-1000/uefa_champions_league_-_2025-26_-_league_phase_fixtures_by_matchday.pdf |title=2025/26 UEFA Champions League: League phase match calendar (by matchday) |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=30 August 2025 |access-date=30 August 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://editorial.uefa.com/resources/029c-1e9a3c931e38-1891f54a4f23-1000/uefa_champions_league_-_2025-26_-_league_phase_fixtures_by_pot.pdf |title=2025/26 UEFA Champions League: League phase match calendar (by pot) |publisher=[[UEFA]] |date=30 August 2025 |access-date=30 August 2025}}</ref> This was to ensure no calendar clashes with teams in the [[2025–26 UEFA Europa League league phase|Europa League]] and [[2025–26 UEFA Conference League league phase|Conference League]] playing in the same cities.

In principle, each team did not play more than two home matches or two away matches in a row, and played one home match and one away match across both the first and last two matchdays. The matches were played on 16–18 September (exclusive week),{{refn|group=note|As part of the scheduling for the 2025–26 UEFA men's club season, each competition had an "exclusive week" in the calendar, with no other competitions scheduled during this week. For the Champions League, this took place on matchday 1 (16–18 September 2025).}} 30 September – 1 October, 21–22 October, 4–5 November, 25–26 November, 9–10 December 2025, 20–21 January and 28 January 2026. All matches were played on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, except for the competition's exclusive week, which also included Thursday fixtures. The scheduled kick-off times were generally 18:45 (two matches on each day) and 21:00 (remaining matches) CET/CEST. The only exception was the final matchday, where all fixtures were played simultaneously at 21:00.<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/0268-12157d69ce2d-9f011c70f6fa-1000/ |title=New format for Champions League post-2024: Everything you need to know |website=[[UEFA]] |publisher=Union of European Football Associations |date=12 June 2024 |access-date=27 August 2024}}</ref><ref name="regulations"/>

Times are [[Central European Time|CET]] or [[Central European Summer Time|CEST]],{{refn|group=note|[[Central European Summer Time|CEST]] ([[UTC+02:00|UTC+2]]) for dates up to 25 October 2025 (matchdays 1–3), and [[Central European Time|CET]] ([[UTC+01:00|UTC+1]]) for dates thereafter (matchdays 4–8).}} as listed by UEFA (local times, if different, are in parentheses).
